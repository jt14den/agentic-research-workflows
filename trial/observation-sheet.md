## Five-minute interview

1. Which step was most confusing?
2. Which step would you use in your own work? Describe the task.
3. Is there a repeated task you would want to keep as a program? If not, why?

## Signs the design is wrong

- Learners repeat words such as "reuse" or "abstraction" but cannot apply them.
- Paths, arguments or syntax take the time meant for checking.
- Learners accept the batch 2 output without seeing any decision in it.
- Learners cannot name any use for this in their own work.
